### Lunch-box packing

For the children's boxes, keep portions separate where possible:

* **Main box:** rice/dosa/chapati/biryani.
* **Small side box:** poriyal, potato fry, egg or curd.
* **Snack box:** fruit, sundal, corn, popcorn or homemade aval mix.
* For younger children, cut dosa/chapati into small pieces or roll them so they are easier to eat at school.

# 4. Three Tips for Children's Lunch Boxes

1. **Make food visually different:** Roll chapati, cut dosa into triangles, or use small portions of colourful vegetables instead of sending one large serving.

2. **Keep one guaranteed favourite:** If the main dish is new, pair it with something they reliably eat—potato fry, curd, banana, egg or a small sweet item.

3. **Avoid soggy food:** Pack curd, chutney and gravy separately when possible. Let hot food cool for a few minutes before closing the lunch box so it doesn't become watery.

This plan also deliberately avoids **bitter gourd and brinjal** and uses the same base ingredients across several meals without making the family feel like they're eating the same dish every day.
